Not your usual hackathon

VibeCraft trades the standard hackathon's dead-serious tone for something more fun to actually be at. You'll build under a fixed clock, make real prioritisation calls when everything feels urgent at once, and pick up efficient AI-assisted building along the way — all while it stays light enough that people actually show up wanting to build.

Technical skill matters, but so does how you handle the pressure. Both get evaluated.

Team & Registration

What you need to know before you sign up.

Team Rules

Whoever you bring, bring them ready to build — how your team handles the pressure matters as much as what you ship.

  • 2–4 members per team — no solo runs, no teams of 5+.
  • Bring your own device. Laptop, tablet, whatever you build on — the venue doesn't supply hardware. Get it charged and your tools installed before Round 1 starts.
  • Technical and non-technical tasks both count toward evaluation. Show up and participate in both — sitting one out costs you.

Tip: sort out who's doing what before the clock starts. There's no pause button for team huddles once Round 1 begins.

How to Register

  • ₹500 per team, one payment covers the whole squad.
  • Registration happens through CampusQuest only. If you expressed interest via Unstop, check your email for the CampusQuest link — Unstop is for event info, not registration.
  • Double-check your name, department, and university before you submit. Certificates are printed from this data, and changes after registration aren't accepted.
  • After payment, watch your inbox — a confirmation mail with further details follows from the official NTT team.

The Rounds

Three rounds. Round 1 is open to everyone. What's past it only unlocks once you've cleared it.

Round 1 — The Elimination

You'll get a problem statement and a fixed window to submit a working technical solution. This round tests raw problem-solving — how fast and how well you can build under pressure.

Non-technical tasks are optional here, but finishing them banks you bonus points that carry into Round 2.

The exact problem statement drops on the morning of the event — this page covers the rules, not the puzzle.

Only Round 1 survivors get this far

If you've cleared Round 1, you've already got the code. Enter it below.

Round 2 — Level Up

Shortlisted teams get an advanced problem statement and move through multiple build stages. Extra points are on offer for the Technical Tasks here — and a few surprises are built in, so stay sharp.

Round 3 — The Finale

Submit your final working prototype and present it. Like the reality shows, strong performance in earlier rounds earns you an edge here too. This is where VibeCraft Season 1 crowns its winner.

How We Judge

  1. Relevance of your solution to the problem statement
  2. Feasibility of what you've built
  3. How well you actually used the given dataset
  4. Overall user experience and solution design
  5. Performance during the Technical Tasks round

Bonus: strong performance in the Non-Technical Tasks gives your team an added edge in the next round.
